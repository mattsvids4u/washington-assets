1 Site/footprint
- Square 690: Independence Ave (north; "B Street" on the 1905-07 drawings), First St SE (east), C St SE (south), New Jersey Ave SE (west, diagonal) [VERIFIED AOC-H; LoC 97506876]
- "hollow trapezoid": four wings around a central court [VERIFIED AOC/MTFA]; closed quadrangle from 1908 [PROBABLE SEN]
- North side noticeably longer than south (New Jersey Ave diagonal) [PROBABLE]; NE/SE ~90 deg, NW acute, SW obtuse [APPROXIMATE]
- Main entrance and rotunda at the NW corner (Independence x New Jersey) [VERIFIED AOC-C "at the main entrance"; PROBABLE MTFA "northwest corner"]
- Rotunda interior 57 ft 4 in diameter, three storeys, 18 Corinthian columns over a pier arcade, coffered dome with glazed oculus [VERIFIED]; exterior dome/drum NOT FOUND
- Court "300-foot-wide opening encompassing 70,000 sq ft", floor originally at basement level; 1908 lawn/drive/fountain [VERIFIED AOC-CY]
- 1955: 308-car garage built under the court; court = garage deck until the Renewal; access via "arched driveway" [VERIFIED AOC-H/AOC-CY] (one secondary says 1958) -> Nov 1963 court = garage deck
- Cornerstone at NE corner, laid 14 Apr 1906 [VERIFIED AOC-H]

2 Dates
- Carrere & Hastings hired Apr 1904 (Hastings led the House building); Elliott Woods superintendent [VERIFIED AOC-H/SAH-E]
- First brick 5 Jul 1905; occupied 10 Jan 1908 [VERIFIED CDIR]; 397 offices, 14 committee rooms [VERIFIED HH-08]
- 1913-14: fifth storey added "by raising the roof", 51 rooms [VERIFIED AOC-H/CDIR]; wings and street appearance NOT FOUND
- "Old House Office Building" after Longworth opened 1933 [VERIFIED HH-NM]
- Named Cannon by P.L. 87-453 (H.J.Res. 711), signed 21 May 1962 [VERIFIED HH-NM] -> "Cannon House Office Building" is correct for Nov 1963
- Rayburn under construction in Nov 1963 (cornerstone 24 May 1962, opened 23 Feb 1965) [VERIFIED HH-NM]
- Do NOT show the 2015-c.2025 Cannon Renewal (court restored as lawn + fountain etc.) [VERIFIED AOC-RN/AOC-CY]

3 Dimensions
- Facade lengths and heights NOT FOUND
- Storeys: basement + 4 originally; + 5th (1913-14) -> basement + 5 in 1963 [VERIFIED AOC; WP]
- Court 300 ft wide, 70,000 sq ft (-> ~230 ft deep if rectangular) [VERIFIED AOC-CY / APPROXIMATE]
- Basement fully exposed on court faces [VERIFIED AOC-CY]; street-side exposure NOT FOUND

4 Facade
- Rusticated base carrying a colonnade with entablature and balustrade, "reminiscent of the Colonnade du Louvre" [VERIFIED SAH-C; WP]
- Independence Ave (north): 34 fluted Doric columns [VERIFIED AOC-DC; AOC-C; HH-BW]
- New Jersey Ave (west): pilasters echo the colonnade ("pilastrade") [VERIFIED SAH-C/SAH-E]
- First St and C St: pilasters continue the Doric order [PROBABLE AOC-DC]
- Base = "long rusticated arcades"; the order rises "through two stories" above [VERIFIED SAH-R, text on the twins]
- Paired vs single columns UNRESOLVED (SAH-R "doubled fluted columns ... doubled pilasters") -> HIGH PRIORITY photo check

5 Materials
- White marble exterior adopted Mar 1905 [VERIFIED AOC-H]
- Independence + New Jersey fronts: marble from South Dover, New York; C St + First St fronts: Georgia marble; court fronts: Bedford, Indiana limestone [VERIFIED AOC-H]
- "white Vermont marble" (WP) contradicts AOC - not used
- granite location NOT FOUND; window frames, roof covering NOT FOUND

6 Russell twin cross-check
- "almost identical", same firm [VERIFIED AOC-H]; both 34 fluted Doric columns facing the Capitol [VERIFIED AOC-DC]
- Russell colonnade on Constitution Ave + pilastrade on Delaware Ave mirrors Cannon Independence/New Jersey [VERIFIED SAH-E]
- House building a full quadrangle from the start; Senate open along First St until 1932-33 wing [PROBABLE SEN]
