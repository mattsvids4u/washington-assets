1 Site
- On 2nd St SE between Independence Ave and East Capitol St; rectangular, west side parallel to 2nd St SE, east side to 3rd St SE [VERIFIED MAPS]
- West faces 2nd St SE (Jefferson Building opposite); east faces 3rd St SE; south faces Independence Ave SE; north faces a private driveway/service area separating it from the Folger [VERIFIED FOLG]
- Folger stands on the north part of the same block (Square 760 north half) facing East Capitol St [VERIFIED FOLG]
- A St SE once crossed the site (pre-construction photo loc.gov/pictures/item/2011648358) [APPROXIMATE]
- Address 120 Second St SE [VERIFIED LoC contact page]
- Entrances: west (2nd St) = main/public; east (3rd St); south (Independence Ave) reached by a stairway with stylised owls and lamps, originally the Copyright Office doors [VERIFIED COLE/LAB23/SAH]. WP's "main entrance north" contradicts LoC sources — unreliable.
- Lawn: a "southeast lawn" exists today [VERIFIED AOC-R]; setbacks/step counts NOT FOUND

2 Dates
- Land authorised 1928; $6.5M appropriated 13 Jun 1930 (+ tunnel + Rare Book Room); total $8,226,457 after 6 Jun 1935 [VERIFIED LoC chronology/AOC]
- Architects Pierson & Wilson; consulting architect Alexander Buel Trowbridge; commissioned by Architect of the Capitol David Lynn [VERIFIED AOC]
- Under construction 1935 (LoC 2011647041); ready for occupancy 2 Dec 1938; opened to public 3 Jan 1939 [VERIFIED AOC]
- Named "the Annex" until renamed for John Adams in 1980 [VERIFIED LoC blog Apr 2014]
- Original copper roof (85 years old) being replaced 2020s -> 1963 roof = original copper [VERIFIED AOC-R]
- Modern glass entry doors/bollards/ramps post-date 1963 [APPROXIMATE]

3 Dimensions
- "approximately 400 feet on the north and south dimensions and 225 feet on the east and west dimensions" (~121.9 x 68.6 m) [VERIFIED STAR36]
- "five floors above the cellar, the top floor being set back 35 feet from the outer walls of the floors below" [VERIFIED STAR36; AOC/LOCAA "five stories in height above ground, with the fifth story set back 35 feet"]
- Central bookstack portion 147 ft x 322 ft; 12 stack tiers cellar to 4th floor [VERIFIED STAR36/LOCAA]
- Today's floor names: Cellar, Ground, First-Fifth; "The First Floor does not have its own entrance from the exterior" [VERIFIED MAPS]
- Overall height NOT FOUND (estimate: main parapet ~70-80 ft, attic top ~90-100 ft) [APPROXIMATE]

4 Facade
- "a rectangle with corner and central pavilions linked by wide, fenestrated curtains terminated by a high recessed attic story" [VERIFIED SAH]
- "planar wall surfaces, sunken window frames, and stylized decoration, mainly variants on the honeysuckle flower ornament" [VERIFIED SAH]
- "Vertically linked window bays alternate with narrow strips of marble-clad walls" [VERIFIED SAH]
- bays per facade, window metal/colour, spandrels, cornice, attic cladding: NOT FOUND; no source supports green terra cotta

5 Entrances/sculpture
- All bronze doors by Lee Lawrie, fabricated by Flour City Ornamental Iron Co., installed 1939 [VERIFIED LAB23/DOOR09]
- "seven pairs of exterior doors" (SAH snippet) = 3 west + 3 east + 1 south [PROBABLE]
- West/east doors: history of the written word (Hermes, Odin, Ogma, Itzama, Quetzalcoatl, Sequoyah / Thoth, Ts'ang Chieh, Nabu, Brahma, Cadmus, Tahmurath) [VERIFIED COLE]; Odin group on EAST doors (Highsmith 2007687080/079) [VERIFIED]
- South doors: physical labour (male) / intellectual labour (female) [VERIFIED COLE/LAB23]
- Owls and lamps on the south stair [VERIFIED COLE]; Highsmith 2007687094/096/055
- Rights of Lawrie doors UNCERTAIN (private sculptor on commission) -> stylised stand-ins at game distance

6 Materials
- "faced in Georgia white marble with a skirt of North Carolina pink granite around the base" [VERIFIED MARB]
- matte finish [VERIFIED SAH]; marble from Tate GA, granite from Greensboro NC [PROBABLE]
- roof copper, flat-seam and batten-seam [VERIFIED AOC-R / PROBABLE BID]

7 Roof
- three roof tiers: lower, middle, upper [VERIFIED AOC-R]; high recessed attic [SAH]; penthouses NOT FOUND
