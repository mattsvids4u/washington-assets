## What was built

`meshes/SM_F02_SenateOfficeBuilding.glb` is regenerated by `source/build.py`. The clean rebuild is
byte-identical; seed 1963.

It models the 1909 Carrère & Hastings Senate Office Building with the 1933 First St wing, which closes
the court. The target state is autumn 1963, seen from the Constitution Ave escort route. It is about
143 × 110 m. It stands 23.4 m above Constitution Ave grade and 30.4 m above C St grade.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Constitution Ave front (Vermont marble on NH granite) | **34 free-standing fluted Doric columns** (E01) in a recessed loggia. Each column has a base, entasis, echinus and abacus. Behind them, 33 bays with two window tiers: a piano-nobile window with a hood and consoles, and an upper window with a surround. Wall responds sit behind each column, with soffit beams between. Below is a rusticated ground storey with real channel joints and keystoned windows. The order carries a Doric entablature with triglyphs and mutules, then a balustrade of lathed balusters with pedestals over each column. Projecting end pavilions have paired pilasters and a solid attic. |
| SW corner (Delaware × Constitution) | Chamfered rotunda entrance. Three bronze door openings with transoms sit in rusticated marble, above a 6-riser granite stair. Above that, four Doric columns *in antis*, then the entablature and a blank attic tablet. |
| Delaware Ave (Vermont), C St and First St (Georgia marble) | Pilastrade: a Doric pilaster per bay, the same two window tiers and a central projecting pavilion. The **grade falls 7 m north**, so the granite basement storeys are exposed with windows. That gives **3 storeys on Constitution and 5 on C St** (E02). |
| Courts (Indiana limestone) | Six floors of windows, coping and parapet. |
| Roof | Flat membrane behind the balustrade, with low skylight ridges over the corridor lines (APPROXIMATE). |
| Windows | About 1,100 real openings with depth, a frame, sash bars (6/6 above, 2/2 in the base), stone sills and glazing. The glazing uses a seeded interior atlas (dark / blinds / lit / lit behind blinds) with an emissive map for the night read (FICTIONALISED dressing). |
| LODs | LOD0 is 168,705 faces. LOD1 is 63,695 (no channels, sash bars or triglyphs; 12-sided columns). LOD2 is 9,087 (solid walls with glazing cards). |
| Collision | Five convex boxes, `UCX_SM_F02_SenateOfficeBuilding_00..04`. |
| Sockets | Grade at the four corners, the rotunda entrance and the roof flag. See PCG_INTERFACE.json. |
| Site helper | `SM_F02_SOB_SiteGrade_LOD0` (optional): sloped apron, Constitution granite terrace, court floor. DC-B01 can drop it and use real terrain. |
| Materials | Nine stable slots (`M_F02_SOB_*`) with generated tileable PBR textures (BC / ORM / N) in `textures/`, also embedded in the GLB. |
