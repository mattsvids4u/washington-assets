## Cannon, NW corner (HABS DC-2, 1976): v001 is wrong in five places
1. **Corner pavilion.** The chamfer is a giant columns-in-antis bay: two columns frame a two-storey arched window with a
   balustraded balcony at its sill. The two flanking pavilion faces repeat it (column, arched niche or window, column), with
   pedimented windows beside. v001 shows a plain chamfer with one small arched window. **WRONG.**
2. **Entrance.** The door sits in the rusticated base at the top of a wide stair rising straight from the sidewalk. The
   base stands on a raised terrace with a solid parapet wall and a second stair on the Independence side. v001 has a door at
   grade with no stair and no terrace. **WRONG.**
3. **Base.** Behind the terrace wall the rusticated storey shows one arched window per bay. The basement is hidden behind the
   terrace on this front. v001 puts basement windows at grade. **WRONG** for the street fronts.
4. **New Jersey Ave pilastrade.** One giant pilaster per pier, evenly spaced, about 17 bays to the far end pavilion,
   rising through two storeys. The lower window of each bay has a small cornice. v001 uses **coupled** pilasters.
   **WRONG.**
5. **Skyline from the street.** At this distance the balustrade is the skyline and no attic storey shows. In v001 the attic
   reads strongly from the street. **CHECK.** It is probably set back further or sits lower than modelled. A distant or aerial
   photo is needed (Trikosko 1960, 2016646402).

**Columns on Independence Ave:** a tree hides most of the colonnade, so the photo cannot settle coupled vs single. But the
corner bays use single columns in antis, the pilastrade is single, and F02 independently read the twin SOB as single.
**DECISIONS row 1 is reopened. Single, evenly spaced columns are now PROBABLE.** Confirm with the HABS colonnade close-up.
