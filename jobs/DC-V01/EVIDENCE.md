## Body dimensions (1963 full-size Ford, 4-door sedan) — published specs
- Wheelbase 119.0 in = 302.3 cm; overall length 209.0–209.9 in = 531–533 cm; width 79.9 in = 203 cm; height 55.5 in = 141 cm (automobile-catalog.com 1963 Galaxie 500 4-door sedan https://www.automobile-catalog.com/car/1963/771410/ford_galaxie_500_4-door_sedan_260_v-8.html ; conceptcarz https://www.conceptcarz.com/s10448/ford-galaxie.aspx).
- Front tread 61 in = 155 cm; rear tread 60 in = 152 cm (classiccardatabase.com 1963 Galaxie; conceptcarz).
- Tyres 7.50-14 four-ply (standard sedan); OD ≈ 721 mm (28.4 in) → radius 36 cm; 14-in steel wheels (wheel-size.com; carfolio).
- Styling notes from the same sources: 1963 nearly identical to 1962 (new windshield shape, added trim); recessed horizontal-mesh grille with dual round headlights; round tail lamps with half-moon lower lenses; Ford 300 = minimal chrome fleet/police trim; Galaxie adds lower-body chrome strip; Thunderbird-style formal C-pillar on 1962 sedans; 17-in steering wheel on manual-steering cars; front bench seat; column shift.
- Beacon class: Federal Sign & Signal "Beacon Ray" Model 17 (c. 1957–60s), ≈ 11 in tall × 9.5 in base (worthpoint/earlycal listings) — APPROXIMATE match for the small red dome in E1.
